## Dependencies that must not be omitted

R11 does not imply R25. R19 does not imply R21. R21 does not imply R22 without homogeneity. R23 does not guarantee stability under every coupling. R24 does not select the architecture by itself. R18 does not satisfy the general complexity contract.

The [corrections](CORRECCIONES_Y_PENDIENTES.en.md) clarify these limits.
