## Important recoveries

Assessing v0.91 in isolation could attribute gaps to the program that earlier work had addressed. This edition recovers:

- from v0.84, the metric assumptions that justify perpendicularity;
- from v0.89, the descent principle;
- from v0.76–77, relative stability;
- from MF-61B, composition through boundaries;
- from MF-57, the explicit question about cross-relations.

These antecedents do not eliminate the problems of applying those results to v0.91.

## Changes of language that must be documented

In v0.72, a difference is presented through a comparison able to distinguish. V0.78 also distinguishes a potential difference that is not yet accreditable. The synthesis uses “potential” and “accredited” to avoid turning this change into an implicit contradiction.

In MF-54, reflection is a signature of responses. In the algebraic branch, it is a normalized rotation or a scaled product. V0.87 also speaks of reflection as an inheritance/novelty pair. These remain different realizations until their relationship is established.

In v0.83, a fifth image outside one orbit requires another orbit to maintain closure under J. It need not leave the same plane: many discrete orbits can coexist within it. V0.84 correctly separates these two kinds of novelty.
