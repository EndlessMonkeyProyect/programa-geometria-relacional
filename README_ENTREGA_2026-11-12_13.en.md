## Verification

All eight verifiers included in these seven folders ran without error under Python 3.12.3. The three-representation bridge used SymPy 1.14.0. Their checks are finite or local as specified in the manuscripts. Running the scripts regenerates `resultados*.json` and `resultados*.csv`; generated outputs are omitted from the repository.

The Anthropic and OpenAI preprints are cited for the local operations examined in the papers. The Goldbach note separates its own identities from the hypotheses in the Li–Liu paper. Each manuscript distinguishes proofs, finite calculations, and open questions.
