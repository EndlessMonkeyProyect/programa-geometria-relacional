# From the inherited seed to functional recovery · O58 v2.1

**Le Matt Ansatz Di Ego** · Endless Monkey Project · Relational Geometry Program · November 22, 2026

The [complete bilingual edition](DEMOSTRACION_HILADA_BILINGUE_v2_1.pdf) contains the [Spanish paper](DEMOSTRACION_HILADA_ES_v2_1.pdf) and [English translation](CHAINED_DEMONSTRATION_EN_v2_1.pdf). The chain establishes Pell and Möbius identities and an exact functional recovery budget. Its equivalence with the Riemann hypothesis uses Báez-Duarte's classical criterion; the required global convergence remains open.

The [LaTeX sources](src/) and [verifier](proofs/VERIFICAR_CADENA_O58_MOBIUS.py) accompany the PDFs. The verifier reproduces 1,648 exact finite checks. The [section concordance](CONCORDANCIA_BILINGUE.md) compares both editions, and the [bilingual editorial note](NOTA_EDITORIAL_ES_EN.md) clarifies one citation and the spectral lemma's domain without changing v2.1.

The [original manifest](MANIFIESTO_SHA256.txt) checks the eleven source files other than itself. The [publication manifest](MANIFIESTO_PUBLICACION_SHA256.txt) also covers that original manifest and the three new guides. Run `python proofs/VERIFICAR_CADENA_O58_MOBIUS.py` from this folder.
