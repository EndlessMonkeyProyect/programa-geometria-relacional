## 1. The narrative premise, recovered [O]

> The rhythm at which processes happen is what we understand as time. The base cycle is the reference; the other cycles appear as multiples of it, or as relations to it.

In the program, this premise continues the clock as a comparison of recurrences, and the metaphor of the moving mirror. **It does not mean that a dimensional velocity is literally a frequency.** Identifying the limiting constant with the speed of light, if that is ever done, is an additional physical bridge.

The ontological progression is preserved: an uncompared unit → a first difference → reflection → recurrence of an own closure → a cycle chosen as reference → comparison of rhythms → measured time. Gravity is investigated afterwards, not as the definition of distance and not as a synonym for synchronization.

## 2. MF-43: an exact discrete construction [T under hypotheses]

For the modes of a homogeneous chain with a local, reversible rule, the model sets

$$
x_{j,n+1}-2x_{j,n}+x_{j,n-1}=-\eta(L_a x_n)_j.
$$

In an eigenmode $L_a u=\lambda u$ one looks for $z_n=\exp(i\Omega n)$, which gives

$$
2\cos\Omega-2=-\eta\lambda,
\qquad
\boxed{\Omega=2\arcsin(\sqrt{\eta\lambda}/2)}.
$$

This requires $0\le \eta\lambda\le4$. With $\lambda(k)=4\sin^2(k/2)$, the additional requirement of constant group velocity on the oriented branch forces $\eta=1$, and one obtains $\Omega(k)=k$ for $0\le k\le\pi$. In the model's units, this amounts to one elementary relation propagated per update. The logical period $P_i=2\pi/\Omega_i$ allows one to compare $P_i/P_j=\Omega_j/\Omega_i$. A step $n$ is not a second, and $\Omega$ is not a measured physical frequency.

## 3. Invariant comparison from the moving mirror [T under hypotheses]

O45 represents the mirror by a focus $X(t)$, a scale $\ell(t)>0$ and an orientation $A(t)\in SO(3)$:

$$
x=X(t)+\ell(t)A(t)y.
$$

This is a change of coordinates; on its own it is not a force. For three unwrapped phases $\phi_f,\phi_s,\phi_p$, their differences $\delta_{fs},\delta_{ps}$ determine $\delta_{fp}=\delta_{fs}-\delta_{ps}$, a relation of rank two. If the angular zero depends on the parameter $\lambda$, the frame has to be fixed, or a connection $b$ has to be used, with $\kappa_i=\phi_i'-b$ and $b\mapsto b+\chi'$. Then

$$
\boxed{\Gamma_{i\mid0}=\frac{\kappa_i}{\kappa_0}}
$$

is an invariant ratio as long as $\kappa_0\ne0$. If $\Theta_0$ is an accumulated, covariant and monotone phase, the relative clock is $\tau_0=[\Theta_0(\lambda)-\Theta_0(\lambda_*)]/(2\pi)$. This does not postulate an absolute time. If the reference recurrence fails, the clock is no longer defined under that contract.

## 4. Two models of rhythm that must not be confused

**MF-43:** $\Omega$ = *discrete phase per update*, with an arcsine formula and a non-dispersive condition.
**Continuous spectral comparator:** $R_i=1/\sqrt{\lambda_i}$, $\omega_i=\sqrt{\Gamma\lambda_i}$; their product $R_i\omega_i=\sqrt{\Gamma}$ is an exact identity of the differential model. These are different realizations. One must not replace $\omega$ by $\Omega$, nor define a relativistic law from their coincidence without proof.

If $T_i=2\pi/\omega_i$ and $n_i=R_i/R_0$ in the continuous comparator, then $T_i=n_iT_0$. It does not follow that every physical recurrence is an integer multiple, nor that its rhythms are identical. Identifying $v_*=\sqrt\Gamma$ with $c$ requires units, universal causal propagation and a physical test; the value $c_{\rm rel}=1$ of MF-44B is a discrete normalization of locality, not a measurement of $c$.

## 5. Unison concordance is not uniformity of identities

GR3 defines binary links $b_{ij}=s_i\oplus s_j$ on K4. Their vanishing triangular parities are equivalent to being able to rebuild the six edges from the star of any focus. There are 8 coherent link states out of 64 possible; a coherent one can have non-zero differences. That is why **concordance** means compatibility and reconstruction, not strong identity or a universal zero phase.

The distinction between identity and distance must be respected. Comparing two closures builds a distance or a relation; it does not automatically produce a force.

## 6. Consequence for the gravity branch [O/H; not a new gravitational theorem]

Gravity is investigated as a physical change in the relations between closures that keep their identities and their relational rhythms. The energy model $V(r,\delta)=K(r)(1-\cos\delta)$ only penalizes the phase mismatch. At $\delta=0$, $V=0$ for every $r$ and $\partial V/\partial r=0$ at fixed phase; therefore that **single term** gives no persistent attraction when the phases coincide. The identity relation, the change of focus and the rhythm have to be part of a broader dynamical contract if gravity is to be explained.

At the present stage, conditioned models and bridges are known, but there is no unambiguous derivation of the constant $G$, of the exterior potential, of the universality of free fall or of relativistic effects from own closure alone. Introducing $1/r$, or calibrating parameters with the measured $G$, is not an independent prediction. The future test must choose its scale and update variables *without adjusting the answer after the fact*.
