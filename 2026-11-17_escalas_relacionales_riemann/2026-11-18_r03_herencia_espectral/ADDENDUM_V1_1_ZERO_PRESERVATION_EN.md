# Addendum to R–01 / R–02 · Version 1.1

## Corollary on spectral zero preservation

**Status:** exact consequence of Proposition 1 (§3) of [Relational scales of the sieve (published Spanish v1.0)](https://github.com/EndlessMonkeyProyect/programa-geometria-relacional/blob/2026-11-17-escalas-relacionales-riemann-v1/2026-11-17_escalas_relacionales_riemann/FORMALIZACION_R01_R02_ES.md).  
**Date:** November 18, 2026.  
**Scope:** finite prime sets $S$; open strip $0<\mathrm{Re}(s)<1$.

Write $E_S$ for the discrepancy defined in R–01/R–02, $F_S=\mathcal M[E_S]$ for its Mellin transform, and $P_S(s)=\prod_{p\in S}(1-p^{-s})$. The published identity reads:

$$
F_S(s)=\frac{\zeta(s)}{s}\,P_S(s).
$$

**Corollary (R–03, Propositions 1 and 2).** In the open strip, $F_S$ and $\zeta$ have exactly the same zeros, including multiplicities; normalization $F_S/P_S=\zeta/s$ is independent of the finite reference.

**Reason:** every factor $1-p^{-s}$ is holomorphic and nonvanishing in the strip, as is $1/s$. The full proof and normalization conditions are given in [R–03 (English)](R03_SPECTRAL_INHERITANCE_EN.md), §§2–3. This addendum records the result **without repeating** the R–03 propositions.

**Editorial insertion:** following Proposition 1, §3. In the results table add: *Preservation of zeros and multiplicities under finite prime references — exact Mellin consequence; no restriction on zero location.*

**Riemann hypothesis status:** open.
