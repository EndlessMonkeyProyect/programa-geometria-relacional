# Independent mathematical review requested — Mirror Cube and P versus NP

The Relational Geometry Program invites review of its investigation of reference, self-reference, relational radius, and closure. The work begins with the mirror enclosure and develops explicit mathematical contracts involving observational equivalence, modular normalization, graph interfaces, 3-coloring, and proof complexity.

The current review request includes **T-EXT-01**, a proof that a self-sufficient signature for a specified family of full-interface exterior 3-coloring queries over $n$ labeled bipartite vertices requires at least $\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor$ bits. This is a **quadratic information bound, not a superpolynomial complexity bound**.

We seek independent checks of the statements, hypotheses, proof, finite verifiers, and bibliographic precedents, as well as responses to thirteen specific reviewer questions. Historical claims such as N1-B retain their stated pending-audit status. We do not claim to have proved $P=NP$ or $P\ne NP$.
