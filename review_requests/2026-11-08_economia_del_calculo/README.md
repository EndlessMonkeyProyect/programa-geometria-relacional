1. **The three-colour grid.** Does the sequence of minimal orders 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 13, 19, 35, 51 for 3-colourings of the w × m grid, w = 1…10, have a known explanation? Each order is the dimension of the minimal linear representation of the count, certified in exact arithmetic.
2. **The proofs.** Nine claims from the report, each with its status: certified, with antecedent, or to review.
3. **The mechanism.** Whether exact signed decompositions with lower total cost exist.
